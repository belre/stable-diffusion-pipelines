## Prompt

Do not generate images. Analyze only.
Task: I want to replace the masked regions of an anime illustration (832x1216, SD/ComfyUI output) with plausible background (forest, grass, white flowers, soft bokeh). Inspect the attachments and report.
Please answer in this order, concisely:
1. For each of the 4 mask regions (region1..4): what is currently under the mask (hair, skin, clothing, background, boundary mix), and approximate location.
2. Is the mask appropriate? Flag regions where it cuts through the character (hair/body/dress) vs. pure background, and any mask edges that look too tight/loose. Note the estimated mask may be slightly imprecise.
3. For each region, describe what background content should fill it (colors, objects, light direction, depth of field, blur level) so it matches the surroundings.
4. Risks if the character parts under the mask are removed (e.g. hair silhouette breaks, missing limb/body outline, shadow/rim light mismatch).
5. List 3-5 candidate inpainting approaches that fit 8 GB VRAM (e.g. ComfyUI SDXL/SD1.5 inpaint with mask grow+blur, Lama/cv2 inpaint then low-denoise img2img, manual pen-tablet touch-up). One line each with pros/cons.
6. List candidate positive/negative prompt phrases for the inpaint step as a bullet list. Do NOT choose among them; I will decide.
7. Recommended mask preprocessing (grow px, feather px) per region.
Output format: English, bullet lists, one section per number above. No design of full pipelines; if something needs complex design, output a self-contained hand-off prompt instead (goal, inputs, outputs, constraints, environment).
Environment: ComfyUI, RTX 5060 Laptop 8 GB VRAM, final retouch by pen tablet, Runpod available.

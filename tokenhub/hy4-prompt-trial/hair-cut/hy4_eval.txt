## 2. Mask appropriateness

- **Overall:** Not appropriate for a “background-only” inpaint. **None of the four regions is pure background**; region1 and possibly region4 contain small background slivers. If deliberate removal of character pieces is intended, the masks are usable as rough guides but need manual refinement.
- **Region1:** Cuts through left hair and probably the hair/knee boundary; **too loose** into the meadow/flowers and **too tight/abrupt** along the lower skin-and-hair contour.
- **Region2:** Cuts through dress, probable hair/highlight and forearm; **too loose** into intact anatomy/costume, while too narrow to provide coherent surrounding-background context.
- **Region3:** Cuts through arm/costume; the clipped circle is **too tight** for a semantic fill and **too loose** if only a small highlight needed removing.
- **Region4:** Cuts through right hair and skirt; the upper bulb is **too loose** into skirt, the lower section is **too tight** around hair, and the right-edge clip plus concave notch risks a seam.
- The white edges are only lightly feathered; several remain too hard for a large background fill. The glowing overlay makes thin strands difficult to localize, so contents and boundaries are estimates with roughly **±10–20 px** uncertainty.

## 4. Risks if character parts are removed

- **Region1:** Breaks the long left-hair silhouette and individual strand continuity; can truncate hair over the knee or nick the thigh outline. Dappled highlights, blue rim light and hair cast shadows will no longer continue.
- **Region2:** Can punch a hole through the bodice/forearm junction, lose the dress contour or a cyan hair strand, and create a missing anatomy edge. White reflections and cel-shading boundaries are likely to terminate abruptly.
- **Region3:** Risks removing the forearm edge, skirt/belt contour or gold ornament; a bright generated flower/bokeh could be misread as a skin or fabric highlight.
- **Region4:** Breaks the vertical right-hair curtain, cyan rim lighting and skirt occlusion, producing disconnected hair tips or a transparent-looking gap at the canvas edge.
- **All regions:** Possible hard seams, scenery appearing in front of the character, mismatched line weight, color bleed, incorrect shadow direction and inconsistent depth of field.

## 5. Candidate inpainting approaches for 8 GB VRAM

- **ComfyUI SDXL inpaint, fp16 with a 768–1024 masked crop and tiled VAE:** stronger semantic flowers/bokeh and high resolution; **cons:** VRAM is tight and it may redraw character parts.
- **ComfyUI SD1.5 inpaint on a 512–768 masked crop at low-to-moderate denoise:** fast, comfortable on 8 GB and preserves untouched pixels; **cons:** weaker detail and more generic foliage.
- **LaMa/Big LaMa or cv2 Telea fill, followed by low-denoise SD1.5 img2img on the patch:** very low VRAM and good local texture continuation; **cons:** smearing or weak object invention over the larger masks.
- **Pen-tablet clone/underpainting, then very low-denoise inpaint only to unify texture:** maximum control over hair, skin and dress contours; **cons:** labor-intensive and dependent on manual painting skill.
- **Runpod-hosted SDXL/Flux inpainting, leaving the local 8 GB unused:** more headroom and potentially better background detail; **cons:** upload/workflow overhead, cost, and possible style mismatch with the original.

## 7. Recommended mask preprocessing

- Values are at native **832×1216** resolution; scale by about 1.25× for 1024×1536 processing. They assume a **manually refined, background-only mask**; applying grow directly to the raw blobs will erase more character. For intentional character deletion, use grow `4–8 px` / feather `6–10 px` globally and expect pen-tablet contour reconstruction.
- **Region1:** First shrink `8–12 px` away from hair and knee; then **grow `2–4 px` on the background side only**, **feather `3–5 px`**.
- **Region2:** First shrink `10–14 px` away from dress/arm; then **grow `1–2 px` into background only**, **feather `2–4 px`**.
- **Region3:** First shrink `6–10 px` away from forearm/costume; then **grow `2–3 px`**, **feather `3–5 px`**; pad `8–12 px` beyond the bottom edge to avoid a clipped-mask seam.
- **Region4:** First shrink `8–12 px` away from hair/skirt; then **grow `3–5 px`**, **feather `6–9 px`**; pad `8–12 px` beyond the right edge for the softer, more blurred background.
